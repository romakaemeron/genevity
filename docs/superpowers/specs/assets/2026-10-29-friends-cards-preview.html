Genevity Friends Cards

Two loyalty cards for the founder's friends: a light 10% card and a dark 15% card. Below is how each looks in Apple Wallet and in Google Wallet. The name and number on the cards are sample data.

Apple Wallet

Store card. The percentage is in the strip image. The friend's name sits right under the strip as normal text.

Google Wallet

Generic pass with a wide banner image and the name under it. Google picks the text colour itself to suit the background.

What to know

Brand font lives in the artwork

Apple and Google only draw text in their own system fonts. Tenor Sans is used for the logo and the percentage in the artwork. The friend's name is normal wallet text, so it is in the wallet's own font. Google also requires one line of its own text near the top, which I would fill with the name.

The QR code sits on white

Both wallets put the QR code on a white tile so cameras read it reliably. It cannot be recoloured or placed on the dark background.

Real devices differ slightly

This is a close approximation of the layout. Exact spacing and the position of the banner on Google Wallet are confirmed on a real phone before anything ships.
